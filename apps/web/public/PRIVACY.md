# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 2026

## Overview

Zivvvo ("we", "our", "us") is an offline-first practice application for the Zimbabwe VID provisional licence examination. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

## Data We Collect

### Account Information
- **Google account**: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. This is used solely for authentication and sync.

### Learning Data
- **Practice attempts**: Your answers, confidence levels, and session history
- **Progress data**: Mastery scores, streaks, XP, and topic progress
- **Preferences**: Your name, exam date, daily study goal, and sound settings

### Technical Data
- **Device identifier**: A random ID stored locally on your device for sync purposes
- **Usage timestamps**: When you complete sessions and answer questions

## How We Store Your Data

### Local Storage (Primary)
All learning data is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. This means:
- Your data stays on your device
- The app works fully offline
- No data is sent to our servers unless you sign in

### Cloud Storage (Optional)
When you sign in with Google:
- Your learning data is synced to our secure Supabase database
- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
- Sync is automatic and happens in the background

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- Personal identification documents
- Payment information (until paid features launch)
- Location data
- Browsing history outside our app
- Any data from third-party websites

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:
- **Google**: For authentication only (OAuth sign-in)
- **Supabase**: Our hosting provider, which stores encrypted data on secure servers

## Content Protection

Our question bank is proprietary content. We use:
- Per-user option shuffling to trace unauthorized redistribution
- Session fingerprinting to identify leaked content
- Copyright notices embedded in content

## Your Rights

You can:
- **Export** your learning data at any time
- **Delete** your account and all associated data via the Settings page
- **Sign out** to stop cloud syncing while keeping local data
- **Use the app offline** without any account

## Children's Privacy

Zivvvo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.
