# Zivvvo -- Product & Marketing Guide

> For non-technical team members. Everything you need to describe, pitch, and market Zivvvo.

## What Is Zivvvo?

Zivvvo is a mobile app that helps people pass the Zimbabwe VID Class 2 provisional driving licence test.

It is **not** just a quiz app. It is a personal study platform that figures out what you don't know, teaches you, practises with you, and then tells you when you're actually ready to sit the test.

**Website:** [zivvvo.co.zw](https://zivvvo.co.zw)

---

## The Problem We Solve

Every year, thousands of Zimbabweans fail the VID provisional licence test. They study the material but don't know what to focus on. They practise randomly. They go into the test unsure.

Existing free alternatives give you a few practice questions and that's it. They don't know where you're weak. They don't tell you when you're ready. They don't adapt.

**Zivvvo fixes this.** It learns what you know and what you don't, builds a personal study path around your gaps, and gives you a real readiness score so you know exactly where you stand before test day.

## How It Works (Simple Version)

1. **Set up in 2 minutes** -- Tell the app your name, exam date, how much time you want to study each day, and how confident you feel right now.
2. **Take a diagnostic** -- A short quiz that finds out what you already know and where your gaps are.
3. **Learn and practise** -- The app teaches you the material and gives you practice sessions targeted at your weak areas. Every question has a clear explanation.
4. **Track your readiness** -- A score from 0-100% shows how prepared you are. You get a band label (Getting Started, Building Knowledge, Almost Ready, Strong Preparation, Exam Ready) so you always know where you stand.
5. **Take mock exams** -- Realistic test simulations under timed conditions with a 90% pass mark.
6. **Get AI coaching** -- Ask questions in plain language and get clear, personalised answers. "Why did I get this wrong?", "Explain this rule", "What should I study next?"

## Key Features

### 1,396 Exam Questions
Covering all 18 topics on the VID test: road signs, traffic rules, speed limits, parking, pedestrian safety, accident procedures, and more. Every question comes with a clear explanation of the correct answer.

### Adaptive Learning
The app doesn't just ask random questions. It tracks what you know, what you're weak on, and what you haven't seen yet. It builds every study session around what will help you most.

### Readiness Scoring
A real score (0-100%) based on your topic mastery, recent performance, mock exam results, and consistency. Five bands tell you exactly where you stand. When you hit "Exam Ready," you know you're prepared.

### AI-Powered Coaching
Ask questions in your own words and get personalised explanations. The AI tutor is trained on the official study material, so every answer is accurate and relevant to the ZVID test.

### Mock Exams
Realistic 25-question timed simulations with a 90% pass mark. Choose Standard, Personalised (weighted toward your weak areas), or Nightmare mode (hardest questions first).

### Gamification
Earn XP for correct answers, maintain daily study streaks, and level up through five tiers: Learner, Practitioner, Road-Ready, Examiner-Proof, Master. The app rewards consistency without punishing mistakes.

### Works Offline
The entire question bank, explanations, and study tools work without internet. No data needed. Your progress syncs automatically when you're back online.

### Cross-Device Sync
Sign in with Google and your progress follows you across devices. Start on one phone, continue on another.

### Smart Study Plans
Based on your exam date and daily time commitment, the app builds a day-by-day study plan that covers everything before test day.

## Brand Voice & Tone

- **Smart, calm, encouraging** -- like a good tutor, not a game or a textbook
- **Direct and honest** -- "You're not ready yet. Here's what to focus on." Not vague encouragement.
- **Occasionally funny** -- a light touch, never childish
- **Never corporate** -- not formal, not salesy, not preachy
- **Never punishing** -- wrong answers are learning opportunities, not failures

## Key Messages for Marketing

### Tagline
> Feel ready before you sit the test.

### One-Liner
> Zivvvo is a smart study app that prepares you for the Zimbabwe VID provisional licence test. It learns what you don't know, teaches you, and tells you when you're ready.

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
> Zivvvo is a mobile app for anyone preparing for the Zimbabwe VID provisional licence test. It has almost 1,400 exam questions with explanations, an adaptive learning engine that targets your weak areas, mock exams, AI coaching, and a real readiness score that tells you when you're prepared. It works offline and costs as little as $2 a month.

### What to Emphasise
1. **"It knows what you don't know"** -- The app adapts to you. It doesn't waste your time on things you already know.
2. **"It tells you when you're ready"** -- A real readiness score, not vague encouragement.
3. **"It teaches, not just tests"** -- Every question has a clear explanation. The AI tutor answers your questions in plain language.
4. **"It works anywhere"** -- Full offline support. No data needed to study.
5. **"Almost 1,400 questions"** -- 10x more than any free alternative.

### What to Avoid Saying
- Don't call it a "quiz app" -- it's a learning platform
- Don't say AI "gives you the answers" -- AI explains; the app never decides correct answers for you
- Don't compare aggressively with competitors -- let the features speak
- Don't promise passing -- promise preparation and readiness

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does it work on any phone?**
Yes. It's a website that works in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox). You can also install it to your home screen like an app. No download from an app store needed.

**Does it cost money?**
You can use the free tier forever (limited sessions per day). Premium starts at $2/month for unlimited access.

**How accurate are the questions?**
The question bank is sourced from real VID test material with validated answer keys. Every question has been verified.

**Does the AI give wrong answers?**
The AI is used for explanations and coaching only. It never decides whether your answer is correct or incorrect -- that's always determined by the official answer key.

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes. The entire study experience works offline. AI coaching requires an internet connection.

**Can I use it on multiple devices?**
Yes. Sign in with Google and your progress syncs across devices automatically.

**How long does it take to prepare?**
Most learners see meaningful improvement in 2-3 weeks of daily practice. The app builds a personalised plan based on your exam date.
